Brightwheel Bikes Shipping and Warranty

Standard shipping within the Netherlands takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 19 euros. Orders above 1,500 euros ship free.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 39 euros. Express is not available for e-bikes because of battery transport rules.

All frames carry a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Motors and electronics are covered for 2 years. Batteries are covered for 1 year or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

Warranty does not cover damage from accidents, modifications, or use in competitive racing.

To make a warranty claim, email support with your order number and photos of the defect.

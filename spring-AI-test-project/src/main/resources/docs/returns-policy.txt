Brightwheel Bikes Returns Policy

Customers may return any unused bicycle within 21 days of delivery for a full refund. The bicycle must be in original condition with all packaging.

Accessories such as helmets, lights and locks may be returned within 30 days if unopened.

E-bike batteries cannot be returned once they have been charged. Unused, uncharged batteries may be returned within 14 days.

A restocking fee of 12 percent applies to bicycles returned without original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is inspected at our Rotterdam warehouse.
